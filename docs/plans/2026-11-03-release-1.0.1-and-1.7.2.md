## 5. Risks and what is not known

- **Licence for the picture set (CDS replied 3 November).** The reply is general, not a yes to our question. CDS says: it has had a **cybersecurity incident** in its Strasbourg servers since the week of 26 October (SIMBAD, VizieR, Aladin image surveys and others degraded, systems taken offline, no date given); Aladin's HiPS are under the **Open Database Licence (ODbL), share-alike**, so a database that integrates, blends or extends the data must also be redistributed under ODbL; the **original producers' terms take precedence** (for DSS, STScI's: non-commercial with acknowledgement; Nightwatch is free, no ads); mass programmatic queries must be managed; and any product using the data must **cite both the CDS services and the original data authors**. What follows for us, for the owner to decide (this is a reading, not legal advice): (1) the credit must name the DSS authors **and** CDS's Aladin HiPS, and it already does; (2) cheapest way to meet the share-alike clause is to **state in the release notes and About that the picture set is offered under ODbL** with the DSS/CDS attribution, so nobody has to argue whether 1,948 cutouts are a "derived database"; the MIT licence on the app code is unaffected; (3) no further request to CDS is needed, and the IRSA/CASDA mirrors carry the same terms. **Done 3 November on the owner's word: the ODbL line is in the release notes, About, NOTICE, `docs/releasing.md` and the product overview.**
- **Two Apple facts not confirmed:** whether a custom-page URL combines with campaign tokens, and which storefront indexes which localization. Both are marked as tests, not assumptions.
- **App Store Connect is the owner's login.** I drive it only with the owner signed in and with their word for each window, and I never type credentials. Nothing is submitted without the owner's explicit say-so.
- **API route:** the read-only key shows review status only; it cannot edit the listing, so listing edits are in the browser.
- **Pictures outside the set** (the 1,948 are the Planner's list at the default field of view) and other telescope fields of view still need a live fetch; with CDS down they show the grey fallback or an icon.
